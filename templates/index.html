How device discovery works

Devices are discovered by probing the local subnet and reading the ARP cache. Brands come from MAC vendor data. The connection column labels this PC, the router, and devices seen through a Wi-Fi adapter; phones and other devices with randomized MACs are marked wireless because their real hardware MAC is hidden by design; this PC shows its own adapter MAC. Double-click a device name to save a friendly label in this browser. The table shows eight devices at a time; scroll inside it to see the rest.
