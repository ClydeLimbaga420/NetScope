How device discovery works

Devices are found by probing the local subnet and reading the ARP cache; brands come from MAC vendor data. The connection column labels this PC, the router, and devices seen through a Wi-Fi adapter — phones and other devices with randomized MACs are marked wireless because their real hardware MAC is hidden by design. This PC shows its own adapter MAC. Double-click a device name to save a friendly label in this browser. The table shows eight devices at a time; scroll inside it to see the rest.

Safety notes

Checks are read-only. Repairs live in the Repair Library; they change Windows network settings and always ask for confirmation first. Winsock and TCP/IP resets need administrator rights and a restart. Only predefined commands are ever run.
